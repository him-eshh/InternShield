InternShield

For students and job seekers in any field: paste a recruiter message, get an evidence-based risk report, and if no red flags are found, the real posting.

Scope said: "rule-based checks + animated confidence report." Now we know exactly what that means: 6 critical red flags that force a score of 85+, 14 weighted flags, 4 capped good signs, a keyword backstop against prompt injection, verified quotes on every card, a quick-questions box that refines the score after the report appears, and a defined outcome for both scams and real offers.

Risk scoring

Critical red flags (any one forces 85+)
  1. Pay a fee to get the job, or buy equipment, training, or a kit
  2. Deposit a check and send money on, or buy gift cards
  3. Deposit, crypto, or "task" payment to unlock earnings
  4. Run, clone, or install code or a "driver," or turn off a sandbox
  5. Reship packages, or receive and forward funds
  6. SSN, bank, ID, or password before a signed offer or over chat/text/email (caution after a signed offer via an HR portal)

Safety rules (evidence, not opinion)
  • The AI only extracts facts. Each fact carries a quote that must appear word-for-word in the message, or it's dropped.
  • A keyword backstop scans the raw text for every critical flag. Final flags combine the AI's and the keyword scan's.
  • The explanation is written from computed flags and verified quotes only, never says "safe," and falls back to pre-written text.
  • Search page content never goes to the AI. Only links and website addresses are used.

States and what-ifs

No sender contact found
The field invites the user to add it. The check still runs, and the sender checks go under "Couldn't check."
Not a job message
A friendly line asks for the full message. No score is shown.
Only a line or two
The check runs, the report says there wasn't much to check, and the coverage line reflects it.
AI or search down
Never makes up a result. Shows the checks that finished, puts the rest under "Couldn't check," and offers Retry.
Unknown detail
The tool tries to find it first. Otherwise it's asked in the quick-questions box (with "I don't know"), and only if the answer would change the score.
No resume
The level check goes under "Couldn't check" with an upload prompt.
What persists
Profile, resume, and theme stay in the browser. Reports aren't saved; Copy report is the only way to keep one.
Rate limit or too long
A calm "wait a moment" message with Retry, and a maximum message length.

Look and feel

Apple product-page calm: one idea per screen. The system font, or Inter. Headlines of 56–72px on desktop and 36–44px on phones, with tight tracking. Body text 17px. One soft-blue accent; muted green, amber, and red for results only. Rounded cards (16–20px) and pill buttons. The theme follows the system, with a System/Light/Dark toggle and a deep (not pure black) dark mode. Quiet 200–400ms ease-out motion, with fades for reduced motion. Icons alongside colors, keyboard-friendly, and contrast checks pass. Shield logo, tab icon, and share image.
